## Thematic Overview of the Literature

### 1. Age-Structured & Statistical Multispecies Assessment (`Andersen-Ursin`, `MSVPA`, `SMS`, `MSCAA`, `CEATTLE`)
- **Foundations and MSVPA**: @ursin1967, @ursin1973, and @andersen_ursin1977 established the size-based bioenergetic and prey-suitability equations, evaluated for North Pacific stocks by @livingston1983 and simplified into operational `MSVPA` by @helgason_gislason1979, @pope1979, and @sparre1980.
- **Stochastic Multispecies Model (`SMS` / `smsR`)**: @lewy2004 formulated the seasonal age-length statistical multispecies model and Dirichlet stomach-content likelihood used in ICES key runs [@ices2021; @ices2023].
- **North American Statistical Catch-at-Age (`MSASA` / `MSCAA` / `Hydra`)**: @jurado_molina2005, @kinzey2009, @vankirk2010, @curti2013, and @trijoulet2019 developed and tested forward-projecting multispecies catch-at-age models across alternative predator functional responses and diet observation error regimes.
- **Climate-Enhanced Multispecies Assessment (`CEATTLE` / `Rceattle`)**: @holsman_aydin2015, @holsman2016, and @adams2022 coupled temperature-dependent von Bertalanffy growth [@essington2001] and Wisconsin bioenergetics [@kitchell1977; @thornton_lessem1978] with age-structured stock assessment and multispecies biological reference points.

### 2. Age-Length Structured Multispecies Models (`MULTSPEC`, `BORMICON`, `Gadget2`, `gadget3`)
- **Nordic Lineage**: `Gadget` evolved from `MULTSPEC` [@bogstad1997] and `BORMICON` [@stefansson_palsson1997; @stefansson1998], synthesized under the EU `dst2` project [@stefansson2003; @stefansson2004dst2; @begley2004guide; @begley2004overview].
- **Applications and `gadget3` AD Engine**: Implemented for Icelandic cod--capelin--shrimp interactions [@taylor2004shrimp; @taylor2004cod; @taylor2007], Mediterranean hake [@bartolino2011], and Eastern Baltic cod ontogenetic diet shifts [@kulatska2019], and re-engineered into TMB automatic differentiation in `gadget3` [@lentz_gadget3; @ices2021].

### 3. Discrete Length-Structured Multispecies Models (`LeMans`, `LeMaRns`, `CSMS`, `CSM`)
- **`LeMans` and `LeMaRns`**: Introduced by @hall2006 and extended to evaluate selective fishing [@rochet2011], parameter and structural uncertainty [@thorpe2015; @spence2021uncertainty], R/C++ simulation [@spence2020], and near-real-time fisheries advice [@spence2021advice].
- **Parsimonious & Moment-Based Size Models (`CSMS`, `CSM`)**: @pope2006, @gislason2010, and @pope2019 developed low-parameter scaling models and Gaussian moment closures, introducing the multispecies yield Jacobian diagnostic.

### 4. Continuous Size-Spectrum Models (`mizer`, Jump-Growth PDE, Statistical Fitting)
- **Empirical & Theoretical Size Spectra**: Grounded in pelagic size-spectrum theory [@sheldon1972; @sheldon1977; @platt1977; @silvert_platt1978; @benoit_rochet2004; @andersen_beyer2006; @cuesta2018].
- **`mizer` Framework**: Formulated by @hartvig2011 and implemented in R by @scott2014, with applications to North Sea reference points [@blanchard2014], theoretical syntheses [@andersen2016; @andersen2019; @andersen2020], and a 10-year review [@pennino2025].
- **Jump-Growth Diffusion & Endogenous Regulation**: @datta2010 and @datta2011 derived the second-order Fokker--Planck jump-growth PDE from discrete prey captures, and @canales2020 demonstrated population regulation without ad-hoc stock-recruitment curves.
- **Calibration & Uncertainty in `mizer`**: Explored via Genetic Algorithms and Parallel Tempering MCMC [@zhang2015; @zhang2016] and Bayesian History Matching with Sequential Monte Carlo [@spence2016].

### 5. Mass-Balance, Foraging Arena, and State-Space Food-Web Models (`Ecopath`, `Ecosim`, `Rpath`, `EcoState`)
- **`Ecopath with Ecosim` (`EwE`) & `Rpath`**: Originating with @polovina1984 and @christensen1992, extended dynamically via Foraging Arena Theory by @walters1997, @walters1999, @christensen2004, @christensen2005guide, and @christensen2024ewe, critically evaluated by @plaganyi2004, automated via stepwise fitting by @scott2016, and implemented in open-source R as `Rpath` [@lucey2020].
- **`EcoState`**: @thorson2024 embedded Ecopath and Ecosim foraging-arena differential equations inside a state-space RTMB stock assessment model with Laplace-marginalized process errors and Dirichlet diet likelihoods.

### 6. Single-Species Length-Based & Integrated Stock Assessment Models (`MULTIFAN-CL`, `SS3`, `LIME`, `SAM`, `TMB`)
- **Foundations & Integrated Analysis**: Classical cohort and yield theory [@beverton_holt1956; @beverton_holt1957; @cadima2003; @lart2022] evolved into integrated statistical stock assessments [@fournier1998; @methot2013; @maunder2013], size-transition matrix assessments [@punt2016], and non-equilibrium length-based mixed-effects models [@gedamke_hoenig2006; @hordyk2015; @rudd2018].
- **Data Weighting & State-Space Estimation**: Effective sample size and composition weighting methods [@mcallister_ianelli1997; @francis2011; @thorson2017] and Laplace state-space estimation in `SAM` and `TMB` [@nielsen2014; @thorson2014; @kristensen2016].

### 7. Stomach Content Data, Gastric Evacuation, and Diet Likelihoods
- **Gastric Evacuation & Sampling Biases**: Mechanistic cylinder evacuation modeling [@jones1978; @andersen_beyer2005], nonlinear stomach-pooling bias correction [@rindorf2004], and prey condition grading [@hyslop1980; @buckland2017].
- **Databases, Raising, and Likelihoods**: ICES Year of the Stomach and `DAPSTOM` compilations [@ices2010stomach; @pinnegar2014; @barnes2008; @barnes2010], `FishStomachs` stratified raising and `sdmTMB` spatiotemporal Tweedie standardization [@anderson2022; @jacobsen2023], Dirichlet-Multinomial diet composition likelihoods [@thorson2017], and exponential-family / Gamma log-PPMR likelihoods [@morgan2022].

### 8. MICE, Multi-Model Ensembles, and Operational Uptake
- **Tactical Ecosystem Advice**: Models of Intermediate Complexity for Ecosystem assessments [@plaganyi2014; @collie2016], cross-model structural comparisons [@tsou_collie2001; @aydin2005; @jacobsen2016; @pope2019], Bayesian multi-model ensembles [@spence2018], and pathways for operational management uptake [@karp2023].
