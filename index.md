## Chapter Structure

This monograph provides a six-chapter mathematical and statistical review of multispecies stock assessment models, length-structured integrated assessments, stomach content observation models, and dynamic statistical calibration methods for size-spectrum models, accompanied by a comprehensive [References](references.md) section.

| Chapter | Title & Core Scope | Key Models & Methodologies Covered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[Chapter 1](01_taxonomy_and_foundations.md)** | **Taxonomy and Theoretical Foundations of Multispecies Stock Assessment Models** | Strategic vs. Tactical MICE "sweet spot"; State-space coordinate systems (Age, Age-Length, Length, Continuous Mass, Biomass Pools); One-Way ($M2$-only) vs. Two-Way (Bioenergetic) trophic feedback; Andersen & Ursin (1977) differential equations and Livingston (1983) nonlinear sensitivity analysis, and how `SMS` vs. `mizer` diverged; Jacobian community matrices ($\mathbf{J}$) and quadratic multispecies yield approximations. |
| **[Chapter 2](02_age_structured_multispecies_models.md)** | **Age-Structured Multispecies Stock Assessment Models** | **`MSVPA`/`MSFOR`** iterative cohort equations; **`SMS` / `smsR`** seasonal survival, separable $F$, Andersen–Ursin size preference, evacuation rations, exact $M2$, and Lognormal + Dirichlet likelihoods; **`MSASA` / `MSCAA` / `Hydra`** and 7 predator functional responses including Pre-emption and Ecosim Foraging Arena; **`CEATTLE` / `Rceattle`** temperature-dependent generalized VBGF, Wisconsin bioenergetics, 12 MBRP harvest control rules, and hybrid predator scaling. |
| **[Chapter 3](03_age_length_and_length_structured_models.md)** | **Age-Length and Length-Structured Assessment Models** | **`Gadget2` & `gadget3`** 5D state tensor $N_{s,a,l,r,t}, W_{s,a,l,r,t}$, MULTSPEC/Jones consumption-coupled growth, **Beta-Binomial** discrete bin transitions (`g3a_grow_impl_bbinom`), Type II/III predation with `dif_pmin` overconsumption cap, Stefánsson's two-stage iterative reweighting, and Baltic cod–herring–sprat–*Saduria* case study; **`MULTIFAN-CL`** robustified normal length likelihood; **`Stock Synthesis` (`SS3`)** growth morphs/platoons & Rosa Lee's phenomenon; **Size-Transition Matrices $\mathbf{X}_{l,l'}$**; **`LIME`** length-based mixed effects; **`LeMans` / `LeMaRns`** and **`CSMS` / `CSM`**. |
| **[Chapter 4](04_size_spectrum_and_food_web_models.md)** | **Continuous Size-Spectrum (`mizer`) and State-Space Food-Web Models** | **`mizer`** complete PDEs, bioenergetics, reproduction, starvation, and semi-implicit upwind discretization; **Jump-Growth Fokker–Planck PDE** and second-order growth diffusion $d_i(w,t)$; **Regulation without SRR**; **`Ecopath`, `Ecosim`, `Ecospace`, `Rpath`**, Walters' **Foraging Arena Theory** ($v_{ij}$ exchange), and **`EcoState`** TMB state-space ODE model. |
| **[Chapter 5](05_stomach_content_data_and_likelihoods.md)** | **Stomach Content Data, Gastric Evacuation Physics, and Diet Likelihood Formulations** | Biophysical **cylinder model of gastric evacuation** ($\frac{dS}{dt} = -\rho L^\lambda e^{\delta T} E^{-\mu} S^{0.5}$); **Jensen's inequality pooling bias** and Taylor correction; Prey condition & `%F` vs. `%W`; **ICES & `DAPSTOM`** databases, `FishStomachs` CPUE raising, and **`sdmTMB`** Tweedie spatiotemporal diet standardization; Multinomial, Dirichlet, **Dirichlet-Multinomial**, Logistic-Normal, and **Exponential Family / Gamma log-PPMR likelihoods**. |
| **[Chapter 6](06_statistical_fitting_uncertainty_and_mizer_synthesis.md)** | **Statistical Estimation, Data Weighting, Uncertainty Quantification, and `mizer` Roadmap** | Master comparative table across 8 model families; Francis `TA1.8` vs. McAllister–Ianelli vs. Dirichlet-Multinomial data weighting; **TMB Laplace approximation**; GA + Parallel Tempering MCMC and History Matching + SMC + Multi-Model Ensembles; **Five-Pillar Architectural Blueprint for Dynamic Statistical Stock Assessment in `mizer`**. |
