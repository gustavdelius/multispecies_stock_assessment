Chapter Title & Core Scope Key Models & Methodologies Covered
Chapter 1 Taxonomy and Theoretical Foundations of Multispecies Stock Assessment Models Strategic vs. Tactical MICE “sweet spot”; State-space coordinate systems (Age, Age-Length, Length, Continuous Mass, Biomass Pools); One-Way (\(M2\)-only) vs. Two-Way (Bioenergetic) trophic feedback; Andersen & Ursin (1977) differential equations and Livingston (1983) nonlinear sensitivity analysis, and how SMS vs. mizer diverged; Jacobian community matrices (\(\mathbf{J}\)) and quadratic multispecies yield approximations.
Chapter 2 Age-Structured Multispecies Stock Assessment Models MSVPA/MSFOR iterative cohort equations; SMS / smsR (Lewy & Vinther 2004) seasonal survival, separable \(F\), Andersen–Ursin size preference, evacuation rations, exact \(M2\), and Lognormal + Dirichlet likelihoods; MSASA / MSCAA / Hydra (Kinzey & Punt 2009; Van Kirk et al. 2010; Curti et al. 2013; Trijoulet et al. 2019; 7 predator functional responses including Pre-emption and Ecosim Foraging Arena); CEATTLE / Rceattle (Holsman et al. 2016; Adams et al. 2022; temperature-dependent generalized VBGF, Wisconsin bioenergetics, 12 MBRP harvest control rules, and hybrid predator scaling).
Chapter 3 Age-Length and Length-Structured Assessment Models Gadget2 & gadget3 (5D state tensor \(N_{s,a,l,r,t}, W_{s,a,l,r,t}\), MULTSPEC/Jones consumption-coupled growth, Beta-Binomial discrete bin transitions g3a_grow_impl_bbinom, Type II/III predation with dif_pmin overconsumption cap, Stefánsson’s two-stage iterative reweighting, and Kulatska et al. 2019 Baltic cod-herring-sprat-Saduria case study); MULTIFAN-CL (Fournier’s robustified normal length likelihood); Stock Synthesis (SS3) (growth morphs/platoons & Rosa Lee’s phenomenon); Size-Transition Matrices \(\mathbf{X}_{l,l'}\) (Punt et al. 2016); LIME (Rudd & Thorson 2018 length-based mixed effects); LeMans / LeMaRns and CSMS / CSM (moment-based length spectra).
Chapter 4 Continuous Size-Spectrum (mizer) and State-Space Food-Web Models mizer complete PDEs, bioenergetics, reproduction, starvation, and semi-implicit upwind discretization; Jump-Growth Fokker–Planck PDE (Datta, Delius & Law 2010) and second-order growth diffusion \(d_i(w,t)\); Regulation without SRR (Canales, Delius & Law 2020); Ecopath, Ecosim, Rpath, Walters’ Foraging Arena Theory (\(v_{ij}\) exchange), and EcoState (Thorson et al. 2024 TMB state-space ODE model).
Chapter 5 Stomach Content Data, Gastric Evacuation Physics, and Diet Likelihood Formulations Biophysical cylinder model of gastric evacuation (\(\frac{dS}{dt} = -\rho L^\lambda e^{\delta T} E^{-\mu} S^{0.5}\), Andersen & Beyer 2005); Jensen’s inequality pooling bias and Taylor correction (Rindorf & Lewy 2004); Prey condition & %F vs. %W (Buckland et al. 2017); ICES & DAPSTOM databases, FishStomachs CPUE raising, and sdmTMB Tweedie spatiotemporal diet standardization (Jacobsen et al. 2023); Multinomial, Dirichlet, Dirichlet-Multinomial (Thorson et al. 2017 linear & saturating parameterizations), Logistic-Normal, and Exponential Family / Gamma log-PPMR likelihoods (Morgan 2022).
Chapter 6 Statistical Estimation, Data Weighting, Uncertainty Quantification, and mizer Roadmap Master comparative table across 8 model families; Francis (2011) TA1.8 vs. McAllister–Ianelli vs. Dirichlet-Multinomial (Thorson et al. 2017) data weighting; TMB Laplace approximation (Kristensen et al. 2016); GA + Parallel Tempering MCMC (Zhang et al. 2015) and History Matching + SMC + Multi-Model Ensembles (Spence et al. 2016, 2018, 2021); Five-Pillar Architectural Blueprint for Dynamic Statistical Stock Assessment in mizer.
